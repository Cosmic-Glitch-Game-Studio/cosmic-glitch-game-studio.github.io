About the Game

Your own crew has turned against you. Stranded outside your UFO with rockets launching and lasers blazing from the very ship you called home, the only way out is up. Leap from platform to platform using nothing but the tilt of your device — precise, instinctive, unforgiving.

The further you climb, the harder the ship fights back. Platforms begin to move, platforms begin to vanish, and the gaps between them grow wider, demanding sharper judgement and steadier hands with every jump. One mistimed tilt is all it takes.

Alien Ascent is a test of dexterity dressed up as an escape — simple to learn, brutal to master, and relentless from the very first jump.
