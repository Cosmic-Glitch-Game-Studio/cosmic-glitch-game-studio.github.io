Spread Shift

Top-Down Shooter • Bullet Hell • Arcade

Bullet Hell Top-Down Shooter Arcade Game Jam

About the Game

You are invincible — or so the galaxy thought. Spread Shift is a top-down bullet hell arcade shooter built for a game jam around the theme of Invincible. Command your spaceship in the centre of the storm as waves of enemies converge from every direction, hurling themselves at your hull. Master your spread-fire arsenal, read the chaos, and outlast the onslaught. The enemies don't fire back — they are the bullet.
